# MASTER FREIGHT TRANSPORTATION AGREEMENT
**Document Reference:** FA-2025-CARRIER-08  
**Parties:** Global Logistics Directorate and Pacific Northwest Intermodal Freight Corp  
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2025  
**Governing Corridor:** Coastal Transit Route `LANE-NW-04` (Seattle/Portland to Midwest Hubs)  

---

### SECTION 1: STANDARD LINE-HAUL RATES
Standard dry-van and intermodal freight transit across corridor LANE-NW-04 is billed at the contracted baseline rate of $1,850.00 per full container load.

---

### SECTION 2: SURCHARGES & TARIFF ESCALATIONS
**§2.1 Peak Maritime & Port Congestion Surcharge Trigger:**  
"Where regional port dwell times exceed 7.5 days or diesel fuel indices exceed National PADD 5 baseline by more than 15%, Carrier is entitled to invoke a Peak Maritime Congestion Surcharge.  
Effective July 15, 2026, an automatic **+30% freight tariff surcharge** is levied on all container shipments originating or terminating along transit lane `LANE-NW-04`.  
This surcharge shall remain in force until terminal container velocity stabilizes below the contractual 5.0 day threshold."

---

### SECTION 3: AUDIT & RENEGOTIATION RIGHTS
Shipper retains the right to audit monthly bill of lading invoices, request demurrage waivers, or reroute freight via alternative inland rail spurs upon 10 days notice.
