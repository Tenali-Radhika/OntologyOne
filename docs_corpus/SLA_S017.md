# SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA) & MASTER PROCUREMENT CONTRACT
**Document Reference:** SLA-2024-VEND-S017  
**Parties:** Global Manufacturing Corp ("Buyer") and MicroCore Silicon Dynamics ("Supplier S-017")  
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2024  
**Governing Category:** Micro-controllers, Sensor ASICs, Embedded Processing Units  

---

### SECTION 1: SCOPE OF SUPPLY
Supplier S-017 is designated as a Tier-1 strategic partner supplying active semiconductors and custom micro-controllers across corporate assembly hubs:
- Plant P03 (Midwest Advanced Assembly)
- Plant P07 (Southeast Integration Campus)
- Plant P09 (Pacific Precision Works)

---

### SECTION 2: SERVICE LEVEL METRICS & PERFORMANCE THRESHOLDS
2.1 **Committed On-Time Delivery (OTD):** Supplier shall sustain a quarterly rolling On-Time Delivery Rate of **≥ 95.0%**, computed against contractually agreed arrival dates (`promised_date`).  
2.2 **Default Penalty:** Any calendar quarter wherein supplier OTD falls below 85.0% shall trigger immediate executive vendor cure meetings, penalty fee assessments of 2.5% on gross invoiced volume, and qualification of alternate secondary sources.

---

### SECTION 4: FORCE MAJEURE, CAPACITY CONSTRAINTS & ALLOCATION NOTICES
**§4.2 Capacity Constraint & Temporary Lead-Time Revision (Issued Q3 2026):**  
"Notice of Foundry Re-Tooling and Fab Allocation Restriction:  
Due to upstream wafer substrate shortage and scheduled clean-room maintenance at the Dresden Fab facility, Supplier S-017 has invoked Section 4.2 Allocation Procedures effective July 1, 2026 through September 30, 2026.  
1. **Lead Time Extension:** Standard order lead time for the 17 active microcontroller part families (including PART-MCU-101 through PART-MCU-117) is unilaterally revised from 14 calendar days to 28 calendar days.  
2. **Buffer Inventory Depletion:** Existing buffer stock reserves at hub depots are restricted. Shipments during weeks 28–36 will experience delayed dispatches.  
3. **Plant Impact:** Fulfilling plants P03, P07, and P09 are placed on restricted daily allocation quotas until full wafer manufacturing resumes."

---

### SECTION 5: ESCALATION & REMEDIATION PROTOCOL
In the event of an OTD breach caused by §4.2 invocation:
- Buyer Procurement Operations shall initiate formal supplier review.
- Expedited shipping options (hot-shot courier, dedicated air cargo) must be co-funded.
- Alternate second-source components must be prioritized for critical customer orders.
