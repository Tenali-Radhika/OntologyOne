MEMORANDUM: RETIREMENT OF LEGACY SHIP-DATE METRIC & ENFORCEMENT OF OTD_V1
TO: Global Supply Chain Analytics & Executive Committee
FROM: Governance & Semantic Architecture Board
DATE: January 10, 2026
SUBJECT: Deprecation of raw_orders.is_delayed column

1. BACKGROUND & PROBLEM STATEMENT:
In legacy operational systems, delivery punctuality was historically approximated using the database column raw_orders.is_delayed. 
Crucially, raw_orders.is_delayed evaluated whether the manufacturing plant dispatched the carton by the scheduled ship_date (dock departure), completely ignoring carrier transit dwell and customer delivery_date.
Under this legacy formula, historical reporting artificially showed an On-Time Delivery rate of ~94.2%, while real customer dock receipt punctuality was significantly lower (~91.4%). Customers experienced late arrivals while internal reports claimed success.

2. GOVERNANCE RULING:
Effective immediately, the column raw_orders.is_delayed (and core_order_line.is_delayed_legacy) is declared DEPRECATED and strictly UNCERTIFIED.
No analytical agent, executive dashboard, or CoCo query may utilize this column to report on-time performance.

3. GOVERNED CANONICAL METRIC (OTD_V1):
All reporting must exclusively query the certified metric contract OTD_V1:
- Formal Definition: Delivered orders arriving at or before contractually committed arrival date (delivery_date <= promised_date).
- Canonical Mart: ONTO_HACKATHON.MART.MART_OTD_AGGREGATE
Any prompt querying "delayed orders" or "on-time delivery" must refuse raw_orders.is_delayed and cite OTD_V1.
