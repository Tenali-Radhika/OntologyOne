STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: REGIONAL CAPACITY BALANCING & FILL-RATE REMEDIATION
Document Code: SOP-OPS-REG-04
Applicability: North American Assembly Network (Plants P01, P03, P07, P09)
Effective Date: February 2026

§1. MANDATORY SERVICE LEVEL THRESHOLDS
Manufacturing plants must maintain an Order Fill Rate (FR_V1) of at least 88.0% measured monthly across assigned order requisitions.

§2. ROOT CAUSES FOR PLANT P03 PERFORMANCE DEFICIT
Plant P03 (Midwest Assembly Hub) has experienced recurring inventory staging constraints, bottlenecked test bays, and supplier component starvation, resulting in chronic low fill rate (<78%). Concurrently, Plant P07 (Southeast Campus) retains 24% unutilized staging line capacity.

§3. CROSS-FACILITY REALLOCATION PROTOCOL
When an individual plant's fill rate drops below 80.0% for two consecutive 14-day cycles:
1. Operations leadership shall initiate a Dynamic Order Reallocation.
2. Active unstarted purchase order lines scheduled for Plant P03 must be reassigned to Plant P07.
3. Common sub-assembly buffer components shall be transferred via expedited ground shuttle from P03 to P07 within 48 hours to restore fulfillment levels.
